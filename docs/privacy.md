# Pahlavan's privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026. This covers Pahlavan's coach for Claude Code (the plugin and the zip) and Pahlavan's Obsidian plugin.

## What Pahlavan collects

Nothing. Pahlavan has no server, no account, no analytics, no telemetry and no ads, and its author receives nothing from your use of it.

## Where your data is

In plain files in the folder you train from (or your Obsidian vault): your plan, your training log, your body record (weight, waist, sleep, the morning answers), your progress photos, the meals you log, and the coach's notes about you (your profile, your injuries and the limits they set, your letters, its decisions). Some of it is health information, and it stays in those files. You can read, copy, back up or delete them at any time; deleting the folder deletes everything.

## What leaves your computer

- **Through Claude Code.** When you talk to the coach, Claude Code sends your messages, and the files the coach reads, to Anthropic under your own Claude account, as it does for anything you do in Claude Code. Anthropic's [privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy) covers what happens to them there.
- **Nothing else.** The coach's engine and the Obsidian plugin make no network requests.
- **A sync you set up.** If your folder is in iCloud, Dropbox, git or another sync, your files go wherever that sync sends them.

## Photos

Progress photos stay in your folder until you delete them. A meal photo you send in chat is read by Claude and never saved by Pahlavan. One saved through the Obsidian meal form waits in `Coach/Inbox/meals/`, kept out of git by that folder's `.gitignore`, until the coach estimates the meal; then the engine removes it.

## Sitting time

On a Mac, the Obsidian plugin reads how long the keyboard and mouse have been idle, to count sitting time. It never sees what you type, and the count stays in your folder.

## Age

Pahlavan is for adults 18 and over.
